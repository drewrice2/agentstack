# Research Memo Sample

## Summary

The provided notes suggest that the hosted option would reduce initial
maintenance work, but the evidence is thin on long-term export costs.

## Key Findings

- Source A says setup time is lower for the hosted service, with migration
  usually completed in under two weeks.
- Source B reports two teams that later moved off the hosted service because
  audit exports were slower than expected.
- The internal notes show no current requirement for custom retention rules.

## Evidence and Caveats

The strongest evidence is about implementation speed. The weakest evidence is
about future switching cost because the examples are anecdotal and do not
include data volume.

## Next Steps

Ask the vendor for export timing on a dataset close to expected production size
before making a final decision.
